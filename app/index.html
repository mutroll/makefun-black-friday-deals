How to read a deal here

  • NEW LOW — the lowest price we have seen for this product. STRONG PRICE — a genuinely good price, but not a record. ROUTINE DEAL — a small or familiar cut; buy it only if you need it now.
  • Every card shows the sale price, the usual price, the discount, the store, when the price was checked and the source that showed it.
  • Discounts are worked out from the two prices on the card — if they do not add up, the deal does not belong here.
